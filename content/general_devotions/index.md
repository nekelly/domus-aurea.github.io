- **The Holy Rosary**  
  The cornerstone of Marian devotion. It grew into its definitive 15-mystery structure (Joyful, Sorrowful, and Glorious) over centuries and was heavily promoted by popes like Leo XIII in the late 19th century.
- **Eucharistic Adoration**  
  A core pre-Vatican II practice. It was frequently expressed through the "Forty Hours Devotion" (solemn exposition of the Blessed Sacrament over three days) and local Eucharistic Congresses.
- **The Stations of the Cross**  
  A mandatory fixture in pre-conciliar parish life. Meditating on the 14 stations was the central communal and private devotion during Lent.
- **Devotion to the Sacred Heart of Jesus**  
  One of the most prominent devotions of the 19th and early 20th centuries. It popularized the "First Friday" devotions, enthroning images of the Sacred Heart in Catholic homes, and praying the Litany of the Sacred Heart.
- **The Miraculous Medal**  
  Stemming from the 1830 apparitions to St. Catherine Labouré, wearing this medal and praying its petition ("O Mary, conceived without sin...") was a massive global devotion, particularly organized through the Perpetual Novena.
- **The Brown Scapular of Our Lady of Mount Carmel**  
  A sacramental consisting of two small pieces of brown cloth worn over the shoulders. Rooted in Carmelite tradition, pre-Vatican II Catholics were routinely enrolled in the scapular at their First Holy Communion.
- **Devotion to the Immaculate Heart of Mary**  
  Closely tied to the Sacred Heart and massively amplified after the 1917 apparitions at Fátima. It emphasizes the "First Saturdays" devotion of reparation, confession, and communion.
- **The Novena (e.g., to St. Jude or Our Lady of Perpetual Help)**  
  Nine consecutive days of prayer for a specific intention. Pre-1960 parish life heavily featured weekly public novenas, drawing packed churches on weeknights for specific saints.
- **Devotion to the Holy Souls in Purgatory**  
  A major focus of traditional Catholic piety, especially during the month of November. It involves offering Masses, almsgiving, and specific prayers (like the De Profundis) to relieve the suffering of the deceased.
